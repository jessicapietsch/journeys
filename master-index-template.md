## 🏷️ Universal Tag Dictionary (Accessibility Standards)

To keep your notes machine-readable and highly accessible, follow these **Tagging Framework Rules** on every file:
1. Place hashtags at the **very end** of your markdown notes.
2. Capitalize the **first letter of each word** in combined hashtags to support screen readers (CamelCase).
3. Use a **hyphen** to separate distinct concepts or sub-categories rather than smashing them together.

### Core Stage Tags
*   `#Stage-GetPrepared` — Prior planning, baseline metric tracking, and safety checks.
*   `#Stage-BeginTreatment` — Active adjustment, high-friction phases, and immediate adaptation tracking.
*   `#Stage-EstablishRoutine` — Transitioning to low-friction background habits and automated supply chains.
*   `#Stage-ReviewOrTaper` — Evaluation, dosage adjustments, or clinical cessation reviews.

### Domain & System Tags
*   `#LifeEventsFramework` `#NeurodivergentSupport` `#ExecutiveSupport` `#MyStory` `#Task-ScheduleScriptRenewal` `#HealthdirectAustralia`

## 🎤 Dictation-Friendly Cheat Sheet (Voice-to-Text)

This vault is future-proofed for mobile speech-to-text inputs. When dictating updates on the go, speak these verbal commands to format your Markdown:

*   **To dictate an active checklist item (`- [ ] Task`):**
    *   *Speak:* `"Dash space left bracket space right bracket space [Your Task Name]"`
    *   *Or simply speak:* `"Dash [Your Task Name]"` (to clean up as a checkbox later)
*   **To dictate critical notices (`**Notice**`):**
    *   *Speak:* `"Asterisk asterisk [Important Note] asterisk asterisk"`
*   **To deep link to physical archives or local URI assets (Omeka):**
    *   *Speak:* `"Open bracket Document Link close bracket open parenthesis [Insert local IP or Omeka static ID URL] close parenthesis"`
