# Personal Milestone & Lived Experience Templates

This document provides two distinct, clean, plain-text markdown templates for your personal digital archive (Obsidian or GitHub vault). Both templates are designed to be human-centred, screen-reader accessible, and fully optimized for plain-text LLM querying (no plugins required).

---

## Template 1: MyStory Reflection Template
*Use this for atomic, date-stamped personal reflections to capture the emotional reality, interoceptive cues, and personal growth of a specific moment.*

```markdown
# MyStory: [Short, Relatable Title of the Reflection]

**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD or Month YYYY]
**Location:** [Where did this happen?]
**People Involved:** [Who was part of this event? (Use pseudonyms or first names ethically)]

### Event Name
* [Name the specific transition or event in plain language (e.g., Late Diagnosis, Passport Renewal)]

### Lead-Up (Context & Triggers)
* [What was happening before? Note any warning signs, environmental factors, or sensory context.]

### Main Experience (Stages & Cycles)
* [What actually happened? First-person, active language. Include cyclic patterns like burnout recovery, changing sensory thresholds, or executive dysfunction flare-ups.]

### How I Felt (Interoceptive & Emotional Cues)
* [What physical sensations, body feelings, or emotional responses did you experience? Connecting to the body.]

### What Helped or Hurt (Pain Points & Opportunities)
* **What Hurt (Pain Points):** [List sensory barriers, social friction, administrative exhaustion, or systemic hurdles.]
* **What Helped (Executive Support):** [List routines, assistive tools like Apple Watch reminders, parallel play, or peer co-regulation.]

### Service Interactions
* [What service providers, apps, or government entities did you interact with? (e.g., GP, My Health Record, Medicare)]

### Reflections or Meaning
* [What does this event mean to you now? Note any personal victories, reframing, or how it acts as a recovery milestone.]

---
#LifeEventsFramework #MyStory-[EventName] #Stage-[ActiveStage] #Task-[GranularTask] #ExecutiveSupport
```
